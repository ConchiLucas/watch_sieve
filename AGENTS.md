## UI work

- Read the root `DESIGN.md` before making a material UI change.
- Keep this MVP to one admin page: four filters, a title table, and pagination.
- Do not copy third-party logos, brand copy, proprietary fonts, or a complete brand identity.
- Verify at 375px, 768px, and 1440px. Check focus, contrast, reduced motion, loading, empty, and error states.
- Test long Chinese titles; do not ellipsize them.
